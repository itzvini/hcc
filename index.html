So… what is the Creature Club?

The Highrise Creature Club is built from two collectible NFT concepts: 11,111 one-of-a-kind creature avatars and the Highrise LAND NFTs that were brought into the same club. Hold either one and you're a member, with perks woven right into Highrise.

The basics

So, what's an NFT?

Think of an NFT as a one-of-a-kind digital collectible that's genuinely yours — recorded on the blockchain, not just sitting inside an app. Your Creature is yours to keep, wear, sell, or gift, and no one can copy or take it.

The club

What's the Creature Club?

It's 11,111 unique creature avatars, plus the Highrise LAND parcels folded into the same family. Members unlock wearable looks, daily Creature Coins, exclusive drops, and members-only spaces woven right into Highrise.

Gen 2 is on the way: every LAND will receive its own Creature, and every Gen 1 Creature its own pet — so Creatures and LAND end up equal in perks.

Membership

How do I become a member?

Just own a Creature or a LAND NFT — that's the whole requirement. Either one grants full Creature Club status: daily Creature Coins, exclusive drops, and the private community spaces.

Your Discord role is automatic. Once your NFT sits in a wallet linked to your Highrise account, your roles update on their own — usually within an hour. Nothing to claim on your side.

The journey

How HCC got here

No spin — just the honest arc of where the club has been and where it's headed.

2021 — THE MINT

11,111 creatures are born

HCC launches as Highrise's first collectible avatar project: programmatically generated creatures, each with a wearable set of items and a promise of perks for holders.

2022 — LAND ARRIVES

A second collectible: Highrise LAND

LAND launches as virtual parcels of the Highrise universe, in Standard and Premium tiers — with daily Land Tokens, room capacity boosts, an exclusive shop, and slime pets. The original dream: custom, monetizable corners of Highrise you could run yourself.

2022–2024 — THE WOBBLE

Big ideas, uneven follow-through

Lots of ambition across both projects, but consistency, communication and follow-through didn't meet the standard holders deserved. We heard the frustration loud and clear.

2023 — LAND JOINS THE CLUB

One family, not two

The original "custom monetizable Highrise" plan for LAND was wound down, and LAND owners were brought into Creature Club benefits. Land Tokens converted to Creature Coins, pets stayed with their owners, Premium LAND mirrored higher-tier Creature benefits at 3×, and a compensation path covered owners who did not want the new direction.

2024 — THE MERGE SHIPS

LAND benefits become real HCC benefits

Regular updates confirmed the practical merge: Discord access opened to LAND owners, Highrise+ eligibility and Creature Coins applied to eligible players, exclusive LAND and Creature giveaways continued, and Gen 2 / shop work moved forward under one HCC update rhythm.

DEC 2025 — THE DELIVERY RESET

Back to visible follow-through

A dedicated artist put permanently on finishing Gen 1 & Gen 2. Bi-weekly updates. Monthly shop refreshes. Quarterly drops. A clear commitment to communication, delivery, and rebuilding trust one update at a time.

Quick answers

Questions you might have

I own LAND, not a Creature — am I in the club?

Yes. LAND owners are full Creature Club members. Your Land Tokens became Creature Coins, you earn daily Coins (Premium plots at 3×), and you take part in club drops and giveaways. Your plot and your slime pet stay exactly as they are.

Is my creature's ownership safe?

Yes. Plans to migrate creatures off the blockchain have been cancelled following Steam approval. If that ever needs to change, we'll tell you early and clearly — no surprises.

How much does a Creature cost — and what if I can't afford one yet?

Prices are set by holders, so they range widely — from a few hundred dollars to several thousand, depending on rarity and traits. For live numbers, the Market page shows the current floor and real sale history. Can't buy in yet? Keep an eye on the Highrise Discord — there are regular giveaways and contests where you can win one for free.

I bought a Creature but don't see my role or perks yet — what gives?

First, make sure the wallet holding it is linked to your Highrise account — that's what connects your NFTs to the club. Once it's linked, your Discord roles and perks sync on their own, usually within an hour. If it's been longer, double-check you linked the right wallet, then ask in the public Highrise Discord.

Which marketplace do I use — and how do I avoid fakes?

Gen 1 Creatures trade on Token Trove (Immutable zkEVM). LAND and Gen 2 live on OpenSea, under the "Highrise LAND" collection. Important: Gen 1 Creatures are not on OpenSea — any "Highrise Creature" or "Highrise Creature Club" collection you see there is a fake scam. Always reach a marketplace through the official links in the Highrise Discord, never a random DM.

Questions you might have

Is this a DAO? Do I own part of the company now?

No. The Council is advisory — a powerful sounding board on creative and roadmap decisions. Pocket Worlds remains the executor and keeps final say on the business, the economy and the brand.

Will big holders just take over?

No. It's one holder, one vote in every seat — never weighted by how many Creature or LAND assets you own. How many you hold only affects which seat you can run for — we reserve seats for different holder sizes so everyone is represented — never how much your vote counts. And 4 of the 7 seats are elected directly by holders, with 3 appointed for continuity and competence. We're stating that split openly rather than pretending it's purely democratic.

What about Gen 1 and Gen 2?

A dedicated artist is on finishing them, and the Council helps decide which creatures and variants make each Gen 2 set — picking from the options the art team can actually build.

What does the Council actually start with?

It starts advisory-leaning, and its "Decides" power grows release by release as it proves out. Think training wheels that come off over time, not everything on day one.

Before you start

Your toolkit

Four things you'll meet again and again. Get familiar and the rest is easy.

MetaMask

Your crypto wallet. Holds your funds and creatures, and signs every action. Get it as a browser extension or mobile app.

Token Trove

The official HCC marketplace. Find the link in the Highrise Discord under official links — never from a random DM.

ETH

Ethereum is the currency you buy creatures with.

IMX

Immutable X covers the tiny network fee ("gas") on every action. Keep a little on hand.

One thing to know

Creatures live on the Immutable zkEVM network. You'll switch your wallet to it when buying or moving creatures — Token Trove walks you through it each time.

Step by step

The walkthroughs

Six short guides, in the order you'll likely need them — most with a community video to follow along.

1
Setup

Find Token Trove & link your wallet

Get to the official marketplace and connect MetaMask so you can see your balances and creatures.

  1. Open the Highrise Discord and go to the official links channel. Find the link to buy Highrise creatures — it takes you straight to Token Trove.
  2. Have your MetaMask wallet ready — extension or mobile app — and make sure you're logged in.
  3. On Token Trove, click Connect Wallet. Choose Immutable zkEVM, then pick MetaMask.
  4. Approve the connection in MetaMask. Token Trove now shows your balances — ETH, IMX, USDC — and your creatures under My NFTs.
2
Funding

Bridge ETH & IMX to Immutable

Your wallet and Token Trove sit on different networks. Bridging moves your funds over so you can spend them. Balances can take 20–40 minutes to update.

  1. First, make sure you have a little IMX — it pays the gas. In MetaMask, swap a small amount of ETH to IMX (around 0.0025 ETH is plenty for most people).
  2. On Token Trove, confirm you're connected and logged in, and that you can see the Bridge button. Click Bridge.
  3. Bridge IMX first. Pick IMX as the token, enter an amount (e.g. 20 IMX), check the small fee, and confirm.
  4. Your wallet switches to Ethereum mainnet automatically. Confirm the two MetaMask pop-ups — the network fee, then the transfer itself.
  5. Now bridge your ETH the same way — this is what you'll buy creatures with. Pick ETH, enter an amount (e.g. 0.025), and confirm the single request.
  6. Wait roughly 20–40 minutes. When it's done, your ETH and IMX balances appear on Token Trove, ready to use.

Remember: ETH buys creatures, IMX pays the gas. You need a little IMX for anything you do on the network.

3
Trading

Buy & sell creatures

With funds bridged over, you're ready to shop the collection or list one of your own.

  1. Browse the collection and pick the creature you want. Before buying, switch your wallet to the Immutable zkEVM network: open MetaMask, select Immutable zkEVM, then refresh the page.
  2. Click Buy Now. Review the small network fee (this is what IMX is for) and click Confirm.
  3. Once the blockchain confirms, the creature is yours. Refresh your balances and check My NFTs to see it.
  4. To sell, open one of your creatures and click List Now. Set your price and confirm the signature request in MetaMask.
  5. Your creature is now listed. Anyone can buy it directly — you don't have to do anything else until it sells.

When a creature sells, a 5% creator royalty is built into the smart contract and comes out of the sale price automatically — worth factoring into the price you set.

4
Moving

Transfer a creature to another wallet

Send a creature straight to another wallet — handy for moving between your own wallets or gifting.

  1. Open the creature you want to send and click the Transfer button — the little paper-airplane icon.
  2. Paste the destination wallet address. Double-check it — transfers can't be undone.
  3. Review the small gas fee and click Confirm. The Immutable zkEVM network confirms the transfer.
  4. After a few seconds, refresh My NFTs. The creature leaves your wallet and lands in the new one. A full page refresh helps if it lingers.

Always paste and verify the address yourself. There's no undo, and no one can reverse a transfer to the wrong wallet.

5
In-game

Link your creatures to Highrise

Connect your wallet to your Highrise account so your creatures, items, and perks show up in the app.

  1. Open the Highrise Discord, go to official links, and click the top one — the Highrise website. Log into your account.
  2. Go to your account Settings and scroll to the bottom, to Connect MetaMask Wallet. Make sure you're logged into the MetaMask wallet you want to link.
  3. Click Connect MetaMask Wallet. When highrise.game asks to connect, click Connect.
  4. Confirm the signature request — it just lets Highrise read your wallet. You're linked, and your creatures now show up in Highrise under Your Collections.
6
LAND

Mint LAND you bought with Deeds

Bought a parcel during an in-app LAND Deeds sale? It lives inside Highrise until you mint it onto the blockchain. Minting is what lets you sell, transfer, or trade it on OpenSea.

  1. Go to highrise.game and log in with the Highrise account that holds your plot. Make sure your MetaMask wallet is ready.
  2. Click your avatar in the top-right corner, then open Inventory. Your plot (or plots) shows up there.
  3. Click the specific plot you want to put on-chain, then press Withdraw.
  4. MetaMask pops up — Confirm and Sign the transaction. Keep a little ETH in your wallet to cover the gas fee.
  5. Once it confirms, your parcel is minted onto the blockchain and ready to trade, sell, or transfer on OpenSea.

Until you mint it, the plot stays tied to the Highrise account it was bought with — it can't move between accounts or wallets until it's on-chain.

Cash out

Turn your sale into money in your bank

You sold — congratulations! Now let's get that money to you, calmly. There's no deadline and no way to "miss" anything: your ETH sits safely in your own wallet until you decide to move it. The whole cash-out happens right here on this site, and the walkthrough below shows every single tap before you make it for real.

🔒Always in your wallet

Your ETH never passes through us or anyone else — it moves between networks inside the same wallet, same address, and only you can approve each step.

☕No rush, ever

The move usually lands in just a few minutes and runs on its own. Close the page, make a coffee — the tracker keeps going and picks up right where it was.

💬We're one message away

Unsure at any step? Stop — nothing happens without your confirmation — and ask in the Highrise Discord. A real person will walk you through it.

Creature sales pay you in ETH on Immutable zkEVM — we move it to Ethereum for you, right here.

The one golden rule: don't send this ETH straight from Immutable zkEVM to an exchange — most exchanges only understand the Ethereum network. That's the entire reason the Cash out button exists: it puts your ETH on Ethereum first, and from there everything works the way you're used to.

After it lands: open MetaMask, switch the network picker to Ethereum, and your ETH is right there (the site's "Show it in MetaMask" button does the switch for you). Sending it to Coinbase, Kraken or any exchange is then a normal transfer — copy the exchange's ETH deposit address, double-check it, send.

LAND sales pay you in WETH on Ethereum — one tap unwraps it to plain ETH, no bridging needed.

WETH ("wrapped ETH") is worth exactly the same as ETH — it's just a different token under the hood, and some exchanges won't recognise a WETH deposit. The unwrap is a 1:1 swap back to plain ETH that costs only gas. After that, send to your exchange as you always would.

Stay safe

Protect your assets

Your creatures are valuable and ownership is final. A few habits keep them safe.

🤫

Never share your seed phrase

Your seed phrase is the 12 random words shown once when you created your wallet — it's the master key. Whoever has it controls your wallet completely. No one — not Highrise, not support — will ever need it.

🔗

Use official links only

Only use links from the Highrise Discord's official links channel. Never trust marketplace or wallet links sent via DMs, social media comments, or sites you didn't find yourself.

📋

Check the address twice

Before any transfer, paste and re-read the destination address. Transfers are permanent and can't be reversed.

⛽

Keep a little $IMX

Every action on Immutable needs a tiny $IMX fee. Keep a small balance so you're never stuck mid-transaction.

🚫

Beware fake support

Real staff won't DM you first asking for wallet access, payments, or your phrase. When in doubt, ask in the public Discord.

✍️

Read before you sign

Every wallet popup spends or approves something. Slow down and read what it does. Sites asking you to "validate", "sync" or "migrate" your wallet are scams — no real app needs that.

🌐

Stay on Immutable zkEVM

Your Creatures live on the Immutable zkEVM network. Only transfer them to wallets on that same network, and don't use a marketplace's "withdraw" option to move a Creature off it unless you're certain what it does — sending to the wrong network means permanent, unrecoverable loss.

🛒

Know the real marketplaces

Gen 1 Creatures only trade on Token Trove. Any "Highrise Creature" or "Highrise Creature Club" collection on OpenSea is a fake — Gen 1 isn't listed there. Only LAND and Gen 2 are on OpenSea. Reach every marketplace through the official Discord links.

HCC staff and support will never message you first. If anyone DMs you claiming to be from the team — asking for wallet access, your seed phrase, or any payment — it's a scam. Ask publicly in the Highrise Discord instead.

If something feels wrong

Clicked a bad link or signed something suspicious? Act fast: move your Creatures and funds to a fresh wallet first, then revoke old approvals from the compromised one, and tell the team in the public Discord so others get warned. Speed matters more than embarrassment — everyone has a close call eventually.

How to submit a change

Four steps from idea to pull request. There's no build step and no framework — just plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS.

1
Fork

Fork the repo

On GitHub, hit Fork to get your own copy of the project, then clone it to your machine.

2
Run it

Run it locally

Start the small Node server and open the site in your browser to see your changes live as you work.

npm start  # → http://localhost:3000
3
Build it

Make your change on a branch

Create a branch named for what you're doing — like fix/mobile-layout or feat/faq-entry — then make and test your edit. New copy goes through the i18n keys in locales/en.json.

4
Ship it

Open a pull request

Push your branch and open a pull request against main. We'll review it with you right there in the PR.

1 / 4

Keep each pull request focused on one thing — a single fix or a single feature. It makes review faster and the history cleaner.

Player Council

Terms of Participation

Acceptance. Voters tick "I agree to the Terms" after logging in with Discord, before their first ballot; candidates explicitly accept at nomination submission. Acceptance is recorded with your Highrise Person ID, Discord ID, and a timestamp.

1. Nature of the Council

The Council is advisory only; Pocket Worlds keeps final say on business, economy, brand and legal. A seat is not ownership, equity, a security, a token right, employment, or any financial entitlement, and is not a DAO membership.

2. Volunteer / no compensation

Participation is voluntary and unpaid. There is no employment, contractor, or partnership relationship. Any perks are discretionary, revocable gifts — never payment or a right.

3. Eligibility

Holdings = combined Creatures + LAND at the published snapshot.

  • Voters: held at least 1 HCC NFT continuously for the 3 months ending at the snapshot.
  • Candidates: meet the bracket asset count at the candidacy snapshot and the 3-month continuous-holding bar; self-nominate in the window by answering the application's open questions.
  • Eligibility is assessed against your Highrise account's current primary wallet (holdings across multiple wallets are not aggregated). Snapshots are final.

4. Voting

One vote per elected seat (4 seats, plurality). You authenticate by logging in with Discord, which we match to your Highrise account to confirm eligibility — no wallet connection or signing is required, and we never move or spend your assets. Voting is per Highrise account (one person, one vote per seat). Votes are final once cast and cannot be changed. Vote privacy: individual votes are not published — only per-seat tallies are. Each voter gets a private, verifiable receipt to confirm their vote was recorded and counted; the receipt is yours alone and does not reveal your choice to others.

5. Candidacy — submission, publication & review

By self-nominating you grant Pocket Worlds a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to publish, display, translate, and process your answers to the application's open questions for the election and the Voting Advice Application (VAA). The tool maps each written answer to a position, but your confirmed positions are authoritative — you review and edit them before voting opens, and your confirmed positions are published. You are responsible for the accuracy of your submission. The full VAA methodology will be published. All candidacies are subject to review of account background and any community reports; Pocket Worlds may decline or remove a candidacy at its discretion, and the reasons will be disclosed privately to the affected candidate.

6. Terms of service & seats

Elected terms run 6 months, max two consecutive for elected seats. Seats are personal and non-transferable — never sold, lent, or assigned. You may resign at any time. A seat left vacant mid-term stays empty until the next election — vacancies are not backfilled during a term.

7. Transparency / no confidentiality

The Council operates openly: members are under no confidentiality obligation and may discuss and share Council materials publicly. Accordingly, Pocket Worlds will share with the Council only materials it is comfortable being made public — nothing shared should be assumed secret or treated as a leak.

8. Code of conduct

No harassment, hate, threats, or doxxing. No vote-buying, bribery, or exchanging anything of value for votes. No impersonation, fraud, or material misrepresentation. No sybil/multi-account voting and no snapshot manipulation. Disclose conflicts of interest. Follow Highrise/HCC community guidelines and applicable law.

9. Integrity & enforcement

One eligible account, one ballot per seat. Pocket Worlds may void votes, disqualify candidates, remove members, or bar future participation for breach — at its reasonable discretion, with reasons published where practical.

10. Data & privacy

To authenticate and check eligibility we process your Discord ID, Highrise ID, Person ID, linked wallet address(es) and wallet-change history (via Discord OAuth and Highrise's internal API), plus the holdings snapshot, vote records (kept private), candidate answers (public), and voter answers (private; used to compute your own affinity, not published per-user). Candidate answers are sent to Anthropic (Claude) so the tool can map each into a position the candidate confirms. On-chain/wallet data is inherently public. See the Privacy Policy for retention and deletion-request handling.

11. Disclaimers & liability

Provided "as is." The VAA affinity score is advisory and not guaranteed accurate. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, a securities offering, or a guarantee of any NFT's value. Liability is limited to the extent permitted by law; candidates indemnify Pocket Worlds for content they submit.

Player Council

Privacy Policy

Who we are

The HCC Player Council election is operated by Pocket Worlds ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices. Data requests/questions: Highrise Discord support channel.

What we collect

  • Account & identity: via Discord OAuth — your Discord ID and the basic profile you authorize; via Highrise's internal API — your Highrise ID, Person ID, linked wallet address(es), and wallet-change history.
  • Eligibility: HCC NFT holdings and transfer history for your current primary wallet, read from public blockchains.
  • Participation: candidate answers and positions (public), voter answers (private), votes (private), and terms-acceptance records.
  • Technical: a login session cookie and basic server logs (e.g., IP, timestamps) for security.

Why we use it

To authenticate you; verify eligibility (3-month holding + bracket); run the VAA matching; record and tally votes and issue your private receipt; publish candidate answers/positions and aggregate results; review candidacies and enforce election integrity.

Cookies

A single session cookie keeps you logged in after Discord OAuth. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we share with

  • Anthropic (Claude) — candidate answers are sent to Anthropic to map each written answer to a position the candidate then reviews and confirms. Voter data and individual votes are not sent to Anthropic.
  • Discord — for OAuth login.
  • Highrise / Pocket Worlds internal systems — identity and eligibility resolution.
  • Public blockchain explorers (Immutable/Blockscout, etc.) — we query public chain data.
  • Hosting/infrastructure — to run the site and store election data.
We do not sell your personal data.

Public vs. private

Public: candidate answers, candidate positions, and aggregate election statistics — most election statistics (per-seat tallies, turnout, bracket breakdowns, etc.) are published on the website. Private: your individual vote (never published), your voter answers, and your vote receipt. Wallet/on-chain data is inherently public on the blockchain.

Retention

We retain election data for an undetermined period — there is no fixed deletion schedule. You may still request deletion of your personal data as described below.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your personal data — subject to legal and election-integrity limits — by contacting Highrise Discord support channel. Where applicable law (e.g., GDPR/CCPA) grants further rights, we honor them.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures; no system is perfectly secure. The election algorithms and the website's structure are open-source and auditable by all users, so anyone can inspect how data is processed and how votes are counted.

Children

The election is not directed to children, consistent with Highrise's policies.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy; all changes are published on the website and in Discord. Contact: Highrise Discord support channel.
